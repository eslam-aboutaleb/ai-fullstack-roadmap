1"""
2SQLAlchemy ORM models for the OmniCare backend, grouped by bounded context.
3
4The flat module list this package used to carry is now grouped under the
5contexts the rest of the application already uses:
6
7  - ``User`` (``user.py``)      -- identity; every other table references it.
8  - ``app.models.claims``      -- claims, their lifecycle events, the pending
9    submission awaiting confirmation, and the embedding outbox.
10  - ``app.models.conversations`` -- conversations, their normalized messages,
11    and the feedback ratings attached to assistant answers.
12  - ``app.models.policies``    -- the ingested corpus: policies, their
13    versioned snapshots and chunks, ingestion bookkeeping, and the versioned
14    procedure playbooks.
15  - ``app.models.platform``    -- cross-cutting system tables: the append-only
16    audit trail and the notification outbox and feed.
17
18Importing this package imports every model module, which is what registers all
19tables on ``Base.metadata``. Alembic's ``env.py`` and ``create_all_tables``
20therefore only need ``import app.models`` and cannot drift as models are added.
21
22Model modules never import from this package -- they import ``Base`` from
23``app.models.base`` directly -- so the registry can import them without a
24circular dependency.
25"""
